# Coaching & Training Sessions

This section is dedicated to practical coaching. I believe that a good training session translates tactical theory and data insights into actions on the pitch. Every drill should have a clear purpose and transfer to match day.

## 🔍 What I focus on:
*   **Session Planning:** Creating structured training sessions (warm-up, main phase, cool-down) with clear coaching points.
*   **Tactical Periodization:** Integrating tactical elements (e.g., pressing, build-up play) directly into physical and technical drills.
*   **Individual Development:** Designing specific exercises to improve player weaknesses (e.g., passing under pressure, finishing).
*   **Match Preparation:** Training sessions that specifically prepare a team for the next opponent's strengths and weaknesses.
