### Corners vs. Goals Analysis
This combination chart compares the total corners generated by each team against the total goals scored. The blue bars represent corners, while the orange line represents goals.

**Key Insights:**
*   **Quantity vs. Quality (The 1. FC Köln case):** 1. FC Köln has generated the highest number of corners in the league (57), yet they have scored only 13 goals. This highlights a severe inefficiency in converting set-pieces into actual goal threats.
*   **Elite Efficiency:** Teams like Bayern Munich (24 goals) and RB Leipzig (25 goals) are much more clinical. They do not need the highest volume of corners to score frequently, indicating superior overall attacking quality and better finishing.

**Analytical Takeaway:** 
While generating a high volume of corners indicates territorial dominance, it does not automatically lead to goals. Coaches must evaluate the *quality* of set-pieces. Teams like Köln may need to shift their training focus towards crossing accuracy and attacking movements in the box, rather than just volume.


## Bundesliga Shot Efficiency Analysis
This project analyzes the relationship between total shots, shots on target, and goals scored by each team in the Bundesliga. The chart visualizes how efficient teams are in converting their chances.

### Key Insights (Conversion Rates):
*   **The Clinical Machines (Bayern & Leipzig):** Both teams are incredibly efficient. RB Leipzig scored 25 goals from 66 shots on target, resulting in a stunning conversion rate of **~38%**. Bayern Munich is right there with them, converting **~39%** of their shots on target (24 goals from 62 shots). 
*   **Wasted Volume (Augsburg):** Augsburg has the highest number of total shots in the league (173), but they only managed to score 16 goals. This results in a very poor conversion rate of just **~9%** from total shots (and only **~29%** from shots on target). They waste a massive amount of attacking potential.
*   **The Outlier (Schalke 04):** Schalke needed 86 total shots to score just 3 goals. Their conversion rate from total shots is a disastrous **~3.5%**. Even from their 28 shots on target, they only scored **~11%** of the time.

### Analytical Takeaway:
Generating a high volume of shots is useless without precision. The league average conversion rate from shots on target is around 32.6%. Teams like Augsburg must focus on creating *high-quality chances* rather than just shooting from anywhere. Coaches looking for a new striker should ignore total shot volume and instead look for players with high "Goals per Shot on Target" percentages, like the forwards from Leipzig and Bayern.
