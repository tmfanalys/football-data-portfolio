# Goalkeeping Analysis & Training

Welcome to my goalkeeper-specific section. As a former goalkeeper, I bring a unique perspective to the analysis of the position. The modern goalkeeper is not just a shot-stopper but a vital part of the build-up play and defensive organization. 

## 🔍 What I focus on:
*   **Goalkeeper Profiling:** Evaluating shot-stopping abilities, command of the penalty area, and 1v1 situations.
*   **Distribution & Build-up:** Analyzing short and long passing, decision-making under pressure, and playing as a "sweeper-keeper".
*   **Save Percentages & Data:** Using match data to calculate save efficiency and evaluate performance objectively.
*   **Specific Training:** Designing drills to improve footwork, positioning, and distribution.
