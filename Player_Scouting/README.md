# Player Scouting & Recruitment

Welcome to my scouting department. In this section, I evaluate players based on their technical, tactical, physical, and mental profiles. A good scouting report goes beyond just listing strengths and weaknesses; it analyzes how a player fits into a specific game model and system.

## 🔍 My Scouting Framework:
*   **Position-Specific Analysis:** Evaluating the exact requirements for each position (e.g., the modern sweeper-keeper, the deep-lying playmaker, or the pressing forward).
*   **Tactical Fit:** How does the player behave in and out of possession? Do they fit a high-pressing system, a possession-based style, or a counter-attacking team?
*   **Data-Driven Validation:** Using metrics (like xG, passing accuracy, duels won, or save percentages) to back up the visual "eye test" and ensure objective evaluation.
*   **Development Potential:** Analyzing the player's age, learning curve, and potential for future development.
