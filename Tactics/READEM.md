# Tactical Concepts & Set-Pieces

This section is dedicated to the theoretical and practical frameworks of football tactics. While my "Match & Performance Analysis" focuses on specific games, this section breaks down broader tactical principles, game models, and set-piece routines that coaches can apply in training.

## 🔍 What I focus on:
*   **Game Models & Formations:** Analyzing structures like 4-3-3, 3-5-2, or 4-2-3-1, including their strengths, weaknesses, and required player profiles.
*   **Attacking Principles:** Build-up structures, creating overloads, and exploiting spaces (e.g., half-spaces, runs in behind).
*   **Defensive Principles:** Pressing systems (high press, mid-block, low block), pressing traps, and counter-pressing.
*   **Set-Pieces (Standards):** Tactical routines for offensive and defensive corners, free-kicks, and throw-ins.
